2. COMPREHENSIVE JURY Q&A DEFENSE (THE TOUGHEST QUESTIONS)
================================================================================

Q1: "Won't safer margins and lower leverage hurt your platform's trading volume 
    and transaction revenue?"
ANSWER:
"Only in Month 1. In a 30-day window, extreme leverage produces a short spike in 
brokerage fees before the trader blows up their entire account. But once wiped 
out, that trader churns completely, generating ₹0 for the next 11 months while 
leaving behind an unrecovered CAC deficit of ₹3,100.
Under MochaTrust, a trader who survives trades 14 times a month for 12 months. 
They generate ₹36,000 in lifetime volume fees compared to just ₹3,000 from a 
churned trader. Trader longevity beats one-off churned volume by 12x."

Q2: "What is your stance on SEBI compliance regarding AI in trading?"
ANSWER:
"Our strict design rule is: 'AI Explains but Never Predicts.'
SEBI rightly cracks down on unregistered algorithmic advisory, black-box trading 
tips, and directional predictions. Our MochaLearn engine makes zero market 
predictions. It functions strictly as an educational structural calculator: 
it breaks down margin requirements, calculates liquidation thresholds, and 
simulates historical volatility shocks. We are 100% compliant with SEBI and 
global consumer financial protection norms."

Q3: "How does a 0.020% fee compete with platforms offering 'Zero Commission'?"
ANSWER:
"Zero-commission brokers don't work for free; they monetize through Payment for 
Order Flow (PFOF) and wider bid-ask spreads that secretly cost traders 0.05% to 
0.10% per execution. 
On a ₹1,00,000 trade, that hidden spread costs the user ₹60 to ₹100. 
Our transparent 0.020% fee is exactly ₹20 with direct exchange pass-through. 
MochaTrade is 3x to 5x cheaper in net execution cost, and sophisticated traders 
recognize this immediately."

Q4: "What prevents Zerodha or Groww from copying this model tomorrow?"
ANSWER:
"Legacy discount brokers suffer from the classic Innovator's Dilemma. A huge 
portion of their revenue comes from high-frequency speculation, margin funding 
interest, and liquidation penalties. 
If Zerodha actively warns users away from dangerous leverage or eliminates 
profitable hidden spreads, they directly cannibalize their existing revenue engine. 
MochaTrade is unburdened by legacy revenue dependencies. Just as Wise disrupted 
banks by exposing hidden FX markups, MochaTrade disrupts incumbent brokers by 
exposing hidden spread markups."

Q5: "How did you arrive at a ₹1,200 Blended CAC?"
ANSWER:
"Traditional brokers rely 100% on paid ads (Google Search, cricket sponsorships, 
influencer promos), driving their CAC to ₹3,000–₹5,000. 
MochaTrust achieves a ₹1,200 blended CAC through a 40/60 split: 40% targeted paid 
digital acquisition combined with 60% organic referral loops driven by our free 
educational risk sandbox and university trading societies (such as RVCE ACM)."

Q6: "Can you explain the technical architecture of your platform?"
ANSWER:
"MochaTrust is built on a 3-tier architecture:
1. Client Layer: React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Recharts for charting, 
   and PWA capabilities for native desktop/mobile installs.
2. Business Logic Tier: A sub-12 millisecond deterministic financial model that 
   executes in client RAM with zero server latency, paired with an Express API 
   proxy and Google Gemini SDK with offline mathematical failover.
3. Infrastructure: Global Edge deployment on Vercel Edge CDN and Google Cloud 
   Run with automated CI/CD via GitHub."
